01 Commitment to User Privacy

At Nightloop Studios, we believe that interactive games should deliver engaging arcade entertainment without monetizing your personal data or monitoring your habits. This Privacy Policy details our operational commitments for Neon Merge Blast 2048 on the Google Play Store.

We do not operate remote user tracking servers, cloud database syncs, or monetization analytics. The application operates as a standalone, client-side mobile entertainment program designed to protect your privacy by architectural default.

02 Zero Personal Data Collection & Transmission

Neon Merge Blast 2048 does NOT collect, harvest, transmit, share, or sell any personal information. Specifically, the game never accesses or requests:

  • Personal Identifiers: Legal names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, or physical identities.
  • Sensitive Telemetry: Precise or approximate GPS location, IP geofencing, or cellular tower triangulation.
  • Hardware & Device Data: IMEI, Android Advertising ID (AAID), MAC address, or serial numbers.
  • Media & Device Storage: Photos, camera capture, microphone audio, contacts, or local file systems.
  • Financial Information: Payment accounts, credit cards, or banking credentials (all transactions, if any are offered in future updates, are handled exclusively via Google Play In-App Billing APIs).

03 Local On-Device Data & Architecture

To deliver seamless arcade progression, high-score leaderboards, and session continuation, Neon Merge Blast 2048 utilizes private, device-sandboxed storage mechanisms provided by the Android Operating System:

  • Android Room Database / SQLite: Stores local high scores, player rankings, arcade session timestamps, and custom player arcade nicknames entered into the game client.
  • Android SharedPreferences / DataStore: Stores user-selected gameplay preferences, audio toggles (sound effects and synthesized synth music volume), high-score records, and powerup inventory balances (Bomb and Zap items).
  • WebView Local Storage: HTML5 / Phaser engine caches game asset textures, responsive UI states, and active board matrix grids on the local device runtime.
Crucial Architecture Guarantee: All local database entries remain entirely confined to the application's isolated sandboxed storage directory (`/data/data/com.example/` or equivalent package root). This data is never synchronized to cloud servers, external drives, or third-party servers.

04 Third-Party Services & Network Access

Many mobile games integrate external behavioral trackers, ad mediation stacks, or remote diagnostic monitors. Neon Merge Blast 2048 strictly excludes these components:

  • No Advertising SDKs: The application does not integrate Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, ironSource, or any ad mediation frameworks. You will encounter zero targeted or banner ads.
  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not embed Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics for Mobile, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer, or Flurry.
  • Auxiliary Local Network Capabilities: Any baseline networking permissions declared by the platform build system (`android.permission.INTERNET` or `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`) are auxiliary to Android's modern internal WebView asset pipeline. Gameplay is completely functional without active internet, Wi-Fi, or cellular data.

05 Data Retention & Your Deletion Rights

Because all gameplay data is retained strictly on your physical device, you maintain 100% control over its lifecycle at all times. You have the unilateral right to view, reset, or permanently purge this data at will:

  • In-Game Leaderboard Reset: You can clear all recorded high scores and session ranks directly within the game settings by triggering the "Reset Leaderboards" function.
  • Android System Settings Wipe: You can completely purge all database tables, saved matrices, and preferences at any time by navigating on your Android device to: Settings > Apps > Neon Merge Blast 2048 > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the Game: Removing the app from your device immediately prompts Android to delete the application's private sandbox folder, permanently erasing all associated local records without residual data left on your hardware.

06 Children’s Privacy & COPPA Compliance

Neon Merge Blast 2048 is a family-friendly puzzle arcade title accessible to general audiences of all ages. Protecting the privacy of young players is of paramount importance to Nightloop Studios:

  • We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies.
  • The application does not collect, request, process, or transmit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in any respective jurisdiction).
  • The game does not include behavioral targeting, social network sharing, direct-messaging chat rooms, or behavioral tracking hooks that could facilitate child identity discovery.

If a parent or guardian believes their child has somehow provided personal data through external correspondence, please contact us immediately so we can promptly inspect and delete any records from our support inbox.

07 Security Architecture & Policy Updates

We leverage industry-standard Android application sandboxing, non-exportable ContentProviders, and locked JavaScript bridge interfaces to protect on-device data integrity against cross-site scripting (XSS) and unauthorized cross-application data extraction.

We reserve the right to revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app enhancements or statutory updates. Any updates will be published with an updated "Effective Date" directly within this document or within our Google Play Store listing.
